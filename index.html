Who we are

A fabrication shop built
around the weld line

Mech Tech Engineering builds and maintains steel structures for plants and industrial infrastructure — boiler houses, sheds, pipe racks and balance-of-plant works for the textile, pharmaceutical, décor, chemical, polymer and power industries.

We run a team of experienced structural steel and piping fabricators, supervisors and welders working in Mild Steel, Alloy Steel, Stainless Steel and Aluminium, holding to the quality requirements our clients set and the delivery dates they need — a commitment we've held since 2021.

01 — Structural Fabrication

Heavy & large steel structures

Structural fabrication is the core of MTE's business: boiler house structures, industrial sheds and balance-of-plant works for textile, pharmaceutical, décor, chemical, polymer and power industry sites.

We work across plain mild steel, stainless steel, high-temperature grades and high-strength steels, and take on complete construction as a single unit or in parts — plus maintenance of existing fabrication already in the field.

  • Complete structure construction — single unit or in parts
  • Maintenance & service of existing site fabrication
  • Reliable on short deadlines, since 2021
02 — Mechanical Fabrication & Installation

Structural steel for plants & buildings

I-beams, angles, channels, flat bars, square and rectangular tubing — fabricated and erected for plants and buildings, plus pipelines, pipe supports, steel pipe racks, platforms and access ladders at our workshop and on client sites.

  • Custom fabrication & gantry cranes
  • Boiler houses & structural supports
  • Balance-of-plant structures
  • Large sheds, stairs, railing & fencing
  • Fire, boiler, oil & water pipelines — MS & SS
03 — Machining

Milling, drilling & spare parts

Milling, drilling, tapping and new spare-part manufacturing — tanks, conveyors, wheels and machinery components — carried out at our workshop in Dadra, DNH, alongside repair of existing spare parts.

  • New spare part manufacturing
  • Existing spare part repair
  • Workshop-based precision machining
04 — Blasting & Painting

Shot blasting & protective coating

Shot blasting and painting of structural steel columns and supports, applying coatings suited to the environment and application to protect and finish every steel structure we deliver.

  • Shot blasting of structural steel
  • Coating systems matched to application
  • Finish work for columns & supports
05 — Gas Piping & Utilities

Design, supply & installation

We supply, install and develop gas handling and control systems for high-purity flammable and non-flammable gases, serving Pharma, Chemical, Food and Pesticides laboratories.

  • Gas distribution, purification & control systems
  • Gas cylinder banks
  • High & low pressure gas manifold systems
  • High & low pressure gas regulating systems
